How you get paid

Guests pay Fichua in full when they book, so every Fichua booking is already paid. Fichua sends you the money minus a {{ '%g'|format(pct) }}% commission.

Example: guest pays{{ example|usd }}
Fichua commission ({{ '%g'|format(pct) }}%)−{{ (example * pct / 100)|usd }}
You receive{{ (example - example * pct / 100)|usd }}

Bookings you take yourself (walk-ins, phone, WhatsApp) are commission-free. After approval, Fichua confirms with you the bank or mobile-money account for payouts. Fichua never asks for your bank details by WhatsApp.
